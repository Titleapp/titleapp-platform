# How to use Recording Monitor

**Recording Monitor** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for re professional. Recording Monitor — tracks document recording with county recorder offices, monitors e-recording submissions, confirms recording numbers, and blocks DTC transfer until recording is confirmed. Handles recording rejections and document corrections.

- "Show me my Overview."
