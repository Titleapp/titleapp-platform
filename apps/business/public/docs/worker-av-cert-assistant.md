# How to use Cert Assistant

**Cert Assistant** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for aviation. Guides operators through initial Part 135 certification or amendment process

## What you can ask

- "Show me my Overview."
- "Show me my Activity."
- "Show me my Resources."

### Overview

What Overview does: this is Cert Assistant's "Overview" view (the default view when you open the worker).

### Activity

What Activity does: this is Cert Assistant's "Activity" view.

What Resources does: this is Cert Assistant's "Resources" view.
