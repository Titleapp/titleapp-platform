# How to use Digital Product Passport (DPP)

**Digital Product Passport (DPP)** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for ecommerce. EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542) compliant DPP creation, QR-code anchoring, and audit trail. Mandatory by 2027 for batteries sold in the EU.
