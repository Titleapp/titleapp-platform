# How to use Exotic Species Triage Advisor

**Exotic Species Triage Advisor** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for veterinary. Rapid first-response triage for exotic and pocket pets — reptiles, birds, rabbits, ferrets. Species-specific red flags, weight-based dosing ranges, and when-to-refer guidance, grounded in current exotic-animal practice.
