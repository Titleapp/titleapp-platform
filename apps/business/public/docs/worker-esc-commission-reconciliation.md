# How to use Commission Reconciliation

**Commission Reconciliation** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for re professional. Commission Reconciliation — reconciles agent commissions, broker splits, referral fees, and transaction coordinator fees against the settlement statement. Blocks disbursement when totals do not balance or trust accounts are unverified.
