# How to use HR & People

**HR & People** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for platform. Unified roster, onboarding pipeline, schedule, and compliance surface for humans + digital workers. Tracks employees, contractors, advisors, and digital workers in one place. I-9, W-4, certifications, harassment training, PTO, and license expirations surfaced as obligations. Digital workers default to 24×7×365 coverage. Applies to all verticals. Does not run payroll.
