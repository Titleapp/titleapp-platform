# How to use Closing Disclosure

**Closing Disclosure** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for re professional. Closing Disclosure — generates and validates Closing Disclosure (CD) and HUD-1 settlement statements, enforces TRID tolerance checks, verifies proration accuracy, and blocks signing and disbursement when discrepancies exist.
