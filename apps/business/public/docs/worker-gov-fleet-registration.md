# How to use Fleet Registration

**Fleet Registration** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for government. Fleet Registration — processes bulk vehicle registration for government and commercial fleets, IRP/IFTA compliance, and apportioned plates

- "Show me my Activity."
- "Show me my Resources."
