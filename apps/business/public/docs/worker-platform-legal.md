# How to use Legal Companion

**Legal Companion** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for platform. Reviews contracts for W-045 enforcement (RESPA, TILA, UCC, state title regs); flags AI-disclosure, statute-of-frauds, securities, usury, draft markings; never provides legal advice.
