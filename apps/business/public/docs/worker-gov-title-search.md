# How to use Title Search

**Title Search** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for government. Title Search — conducts grantor/grantee index searches, builds chain of title, identifies encumbrances, and generates title abstracts from recorded documents

## What you can ask

- "Show me my Overview."
- "Show me my Activity."
- "Show me my Resources."

### Overview

What Overview does: this is Title Search's "Overview" view (the default view when you open the worker).

### Activity

What Activity does: this is Title Search's "Activity" view.

### Resources

What Resources does: this is Title Search's "Resources" view.
