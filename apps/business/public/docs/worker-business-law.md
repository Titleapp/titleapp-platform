# How to use Business Law

**Business Law** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for legal. Paralegal-grade business law worker. Generates entity documents (formation, governance, dissolution), tracks filings + deadlines with the SoS, orchestrates signatures via Dropbox Sign, and stores everything with an audit trail. Operates under user-counsel attestation — TitleApp is a platform, not a law firm.
