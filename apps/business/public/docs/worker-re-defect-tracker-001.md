# How to use Defect Tracker

**Defect Tracker** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for real estate. Logs and classifies title defects (P0/P1/P2) with curative action steps. P0 defects block final commitment until cured.

## What you can ask

- "Show me my Open Defects."
- "Show me my Curative."
- "Show me my Defect History."
