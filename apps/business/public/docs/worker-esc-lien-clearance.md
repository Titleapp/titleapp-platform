# How to use Lien Clearance

**Lien Clearance** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for re professional. Lien Clearance — tracks existing liens on the property, coordinates payoff demands, monitors release recordings, and blocks DTC transfer and closing until all liens are satisfied and released.
