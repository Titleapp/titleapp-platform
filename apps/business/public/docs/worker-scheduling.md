# How to use Scheduling

**Scheduling** is a Digital Worker on SOCIII for general. Cross-vertical scheduling worker. Coordinates calendars, events, and appointments via Google Calendar (Workspace Integration Layer). Domain-specific behavior layers in per use case — RE adds buyer driving-time optimization, Aviation adds duty-time and currency integration, Healthcare adds patient-visit and intake routing, Legal adds consultation booking.
