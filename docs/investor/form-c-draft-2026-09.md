## PART I — COMPANY DESCRIPTION

### What We Do

SOCIII builds AI-powered Digital Workers for industries where expertise is expensive, compliance is non-negotiable, and the cost of being wrong is high.

Think of a Digital Worker as a specialized AI team member who already knows your industry's rules — whether that's a real estate transaction, an FAA regulation, a university accreditation standard, or a commercial mortgage structure. They don't need to be trained. They don't call in sick. And they cost a fraction of what a human specialist does.

We are not a chatbot company. We are an AI infrastructure company. The distinction matters: our platform enforces business rules before any AI output reaches a user, creates immutable records of every transaction and decision, and operates across multiple industries from a single architecture. We have six patent applications protecting this approach.

---

### The Problem

Every specialized business — a real estate brokerage, a flight school, a university nursing program, a commercial property manager — faces the same problem: the expertise they need is either unavailable or prohibitively expensive.

A real estate agent who wants unbiased, fiduciary-grade market analysis can't afford a dedicated analyst. A nursing school that needs to track clinical evaluations, student competencies, and accreditation records is using paper forms and spreadsheets. A charter aviation operator managing pilot currency, maintenance logs, and regulatory compliance is juggling six separate systems. A CRE investor trying to analyze a CMBS loan stack has to pay $20,000 a year for a data subscription and still do the analysis manually.

The result: smaller firms operate without the tools their larger competitors take for granted. And when they try to use generic AI tools, they get generic answers — with no accountability, no audit trail, and no compliance enforcement.

---

### Our Solution

SOCIII's Digital Workers combine AI reasoning with a rules engine that encodes each industry's actual standards. Every output is validated against those rules before the user sees it. Every action is recorded in an append-only ledger — immutable, auditable, and tamper-proof. Every worker knows its vertical deeply, not because we trained a model on it, but because the rules are built in.

**Current verticals in production:**

**Education** — Student learning records, clinical evaluations, CE tracking, and accreditation documentation. Designed for nursing programs, professional certification bodies, and university continuing education departments.
*Pricing: $99/mo + $5 per active student.*

**Commercial Real Estate** — Deal analysis, CMBS loan data (via Trepp and CoStar integration), comparable transactions, maturity wall analysis. Built for CRE operators, student housing developers, and investment firms.

**Residential Real Estate Advocate** — A pure-fiduciary AI agent for buyers and sellers. No commission conflict. No upsell. Negotiation support, market analysis, transaction management, and title workflow — all in one place.

**Aviation** — Pilot currency tracking, maintenance logs, flight planning, and regulatory compliance for Part 91 and Part 135 operators.

**Investor Relations** — Cap table management, 409A valuation, 83(b) tracking, fundraise pipeline, investor communications, and governance. Designed to replace Carta and Pulley for early-stage companies.

---

### Why This Is Different

**Gross margins that don't degrade at scale.**
Traditional software companies hire support teams as they grow. SOCIII's Digital Workers handle onboarding, support, and account management. Our four-person global support team provides 24/7 coverage. The marginal cost of adding a customer is infrastructure — roughly $1–2 per month. At scale, our gross margin approaches 95%.

**A moat that isn't a prompt.**
Any competitor can build a chatbot. Very few can replicate a rules engine that enforces compliance, an append-only record architecture that creates defensible audit trails, and a patent portfolio protecting both. We have six patent applications filed covering these core innovations.

**Workers run the business.**
We use our own platform internally — accounting, HR, marketing, investor relations, and legal tracking are all managed by SOCIII Digital Workers. This is both a proof of concept and a structural cost advantage.

**No HR ceiling.**
Because Digital Workers handle what would otherwise require implementation teams, customer success reps, and support staff, the Company's headcount growth curve is approximately flat relative to customer count. We are not a services business dressed as a software company.

## PART VII — RISK FACTORS

*The following is an abbreviated risk factor summary. The full Form C will include expanded risk disclosures as required by SEC Regulation Crowdfunding. Investors should read all risk factors carefully before investing.*

**Business and Operating Risks**
- Pre-revenue company as of the date of this filing; no assurance of profitability or positive cash flow.
- Early-stage company incorporated in May 2026 with a limited operating history.
- Dependence on key personnel, particularly the Founder/CEO.
- Ability to execute on the business plan is contingent on successful deployment of offering proceeds.

**Market and Competitive Risks**
- Highly competitive market; incumbents include Carta (IR/cap table), CoStar (CRE analytics), ForeFlight (aviation), Canvas LMS (education), and numerous general-purpose AI assistants.
- Larger competitors have significantly greater resources and established customer relationships.
- AI technology evolves rapidly; current competitive advantages may erode.

**Technology Risks**
- Dependence on third-party AI model providers (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT). Changes to model pricing, availability, or terms of service could materially affect the Company.
- Six patent applications are pending; no guarantee of issuance or enforceability.
- Platform relies on third-party cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase, Cloudflare); outages or pricing changes present operational risk.

**Regulatory Risks**
- The Company operates in regulated verticals (real estate, aviation, education, financial services). New regulations or enforcement actions could restrict the platform's capabilities.
- AI regulation is evolving rapidly at federal and state levels; compliance requirements may increase materially.
- Education vertical requires ongoing FERPA compliance; any breach could result in regulatory action and reputational harm.

**Investment Risks**
- This offering is made under Regulation Crowdfunding (17 CFR § 227). Investment in early-stage companies carries significant risk of total loss.
- Shares in the Company are illiquid. There is no established trading market and no assurance that one will develop.
- Investors will experience dilution in future financing rounds.
