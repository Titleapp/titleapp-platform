# Creator SDK

The SOCIII Creator SDK lets domain experts — nurses, pilots, real estate attorneys, teachers — build AI workers that run on the SOCIII platform. You bring the domain knowledge; the platform handles infrastructure, billing, auth, records, and delivery.

## What you're building

A **Digital Worker** is a governed AI agent with:
- A **canvas** — tabs the user sees with real data
- A **chat interface** — Alex (or your custom AI) running on your rules
- **Records** — append-only, owned by the user, stored in the Vault
- **Rules** — your domain logic, enforced at the platform level

## Who this is for

You don't need to be an engineer. The SDK is designed for domain experts who can describe their workflow clearly. Your AI (Claude Code) does the coding; you do the specification and review.

Ruthie Clearwater (nursing) and Scott Eschelman (commercial real estate) are both building workers without prior software engineering experience.
