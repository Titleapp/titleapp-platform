## Slide 2: The Conflict Problem

**Your agent is legally your fiduciary. They get paid when you close.**

- Commission is a percentage of sale price — higher price, bigger check
- Agent earns nothing if the deal doesn't close, regardless of whether it should
- Most buyers sign a 40-page contract in a 12-minute DocuSign session
- "It's standard" is not the same as "it protects you"

*This isn't about bad actors. It's a structural conflict built into the industry.*

## Slide 3: Current State — Opacity, Manual Searches, Burned Records

**Three systems that have barely changed since 1980.**

**Title search:** Human drives to county courthouse. Reads microfilm. Misses the 1987 lien that was released but not recorded as released.

**Zoning:** PDF on a city website. Last updated 2019. Getting a straight answer requires a call to city planning that takes a week.

**The Lahaina case:** 2023 fire destroyed thousands of homes. Homeowners couldn't prove ownership — their documents burned with their houses. Rebuilding required records that no longer existed.

*When records are in a filing cabinet, they're only as secure as the filing cabinet.*
