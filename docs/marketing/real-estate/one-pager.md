# SOCIII Real Estate

## Real Estate Intelligence That Works for You

Buying property is one of the largest decisions you'll make. The data to make it well — chain of title, zoning, permit history, comparable sales, contract terms in plain English — exists. It's just fragmented across county recorder websites, city planning PDFs, and a 40-page purchase agreement your agent calls "standard."

SOCIII gives buyers, sellers, and investors a single AI that assembles the full picture. No commission stake. No agenda. Just the information you need to decide.

Real estate agents are paid when a deal closes. That structure creates a natural tension — not with any individual agent, but with the information flow. SOCIII fills the gap: contract review that explains what you're actually agreeing to, a title chain that surfaces encumbrances before close rather than after, zoning data that gives you straight answers instead of a PDF scavenger hunt.

## What SOCIII Does

SOCIII gives buyers and investors a fiduciary AI — one that has no commission stake in whether you close.

**Real Estate Advocate.** AI-powered contract review in plain English. Flags red flags. Explains what you're agreeing to. No agenda beyond helping you understand the deal.

**Title and Ownership Worker.** Parcel-anchored chain of title — ownership history, liens, easements, encumbrances — looked up by address. Every ownership event is an immutable record. When records matter most (a fire, a dispute, a death), the chain is intact.

**Zoning and Land Use Worker.** What can be built on this parcel. What's restricted. Current zoning classification. Variance history. Straight answers, not a PDF scavenger hunt.

**Property Intelligence Worker.** ATTOM-powered comps, tax assessment, school districts, flood zone, permit history — all in one place before you make an offer.
