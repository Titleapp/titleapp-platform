## Your Products, Fully Documented. Your EU Market, Secured.

The EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542 creates a new standard: every battery and battery-containing product sold in the EU must carry a machine-readable Digital Product Passport — a complete, QR-accessible record of materials, compliance, and lifecycle. Mandatory by February 2027.

### What SOCIII Does

- **Immutable product record.** Every product in SOCIII gets an append-only Digital Product Passport: materials, manufacturing certifications, compliance documentation, repair history, end-of-life status. Records are written once and never overwritten — the regulatory standard for DPP data integrity.
- **QR-accessible public record.** Each DPP is accessible via a scannable QR code — the EU Battery Regulation requirement. Customs, retailers, and consumers can verify compliance without an account.
- **Machine-readable output.** Structured data exports meet the EU's machine-readable format requirements. One click to generate the documentation your customs broker needs.
- **ESPR-ready.** The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation will extend DPP requirements to apparel, electronics, and furniture by 2027–2030. SOCIII's record model covers all regulated categories under one system.
