## Slide 2: The Regulation

**EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542: What it actually requires.**

A Digital Product Passport is not a PDF data sheet. The regulation specifies:

- **Machine-readable format** — accessible via QR code; structured data, not a document
- **Supply chain traceability** — manufacturer origin, critical raw materials (cobalt, lithium), sourcing documentation
- **Compliance documentation** — CE marking, safety certifications, test reports linked to the product record
- **Carbon footprint declaration** — for industrial and EV batteries; lighter-duty requirements phase in
- **Repair + end-of-life information** — repairability instructions and recycling guidance
- **Ten-year persistence** — the record must remain accessible for a decade after the product is placed on the market

Applies to: all battery-containing products sold in the EU — regardless of where they are manufactured.

**The strategic question is not "do we need a DPP?" It is "do we want to build this infrastructure four times?"**

SOCIII's record model covers all regulated categories. Companies that build DPP infrastructure for battery compliance will be ready for each subsequent ESPR mandate with minimal additional work.
