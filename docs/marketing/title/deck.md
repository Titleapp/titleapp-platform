## Slide 3: The Risk — E&O, Missed Liens, Last-Minute Kills

**Three ways the current system fails. All preventable.**

**The last-minute closing kill:** Missing lien release surfaces Thursday afternoon. Friday closing doesn't happen. Buyers gave notice at their apartment. The release was in the chain from day one. Nobody looked.

**The missed encumbrance:** Attorney reviews manually, under time pressure, across fourteen open files. One missed easement. One E&O claim. For a small independent, that's an existential event.

**The "where are we" problem:** Buyers and sellers call three times a day. Processors are managing a stack. The answer is always "let me check and call you back." Every call costs time. Every delay costs trust.

*None of this is the staff's fault. The software doesn't do it. So the staff does.*

## Slide 6: Chain of Title Record + Patent

**Append-only. Immutable. Cryptographically anchored.**

Every title event is written once:
- Conveyance, lien, release, easement — all permanent records
- Sequence is fixed; nothing is overwritten
- Gaps appear as gaps, not as absences that go unnoticed

**US Patent 64/073,700** — the append-only record model applied to chain of title. In production.

**Why this matters:**
- The Lahaina fire destroyed paper records. Parcel-anchored append-only records survive.
- Ownership disputes require unambiguous sequence. The append-only chain is the unambiguous sequence.
- Legacy vendors cannot replicate this architecture. It requires a fundamental rethinking of the data model.

**The acquisition angle:** Stewart Title (STC) is the largest title underwriter in the U.S. SOCIII builds the AI infrastructure layer that STC would need to acquire to modernize its independent agent network.
