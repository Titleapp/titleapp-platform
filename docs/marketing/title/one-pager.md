## What SOCIII Does

SOCIII is a modern AI layer built specifically for independent title companies.

**Title Abstract Worker.** AI-assisted title search that surfaces encumbrances, liens, gaps, and clouds on title faster than a manual review. Flags anomalies before they become closing-day emergencies.

**Append-Only Chain of Title.** Every title event — conveyance, lien, release, easement — is an immutable record, cryptographically anchored. The chain is complete by construction. Gaps are visible before they become problems.

**Closing Package Generator.** AI assembles the closing package from your title work, lien releases, and settlement statement. Attorney reviews and approves with identity-anchored sign-off. Less assembly time. Fewer missed items.

**Client Portal.** Buyer and seller can see exactly where their closing stands, in real time. No more "call the title company." Status is visible. Questions get answered before they become calls.
