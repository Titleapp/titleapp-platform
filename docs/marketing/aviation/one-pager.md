# SOCIII Aviation — One Pager

## Your Records Should Fly With You

Aviation demands precision. Every flight, every inspection, every sign-off is a record — and that record is the proof that you did the work, met the standard, and are ready to go again. Today those records live in logbooks, binders, and filing cabinets. They stay behind when a pilot moves to a new operator. They require a human to assemble before every dispatch decision.

There's a better way to run a compliant operation.

---

## What SOCIII Does

**CoPilot** — One preflight view: weather, NOTAMs, W&B, FRAT, go/no-go. Digital flight log. Currency alerts that fire before dispatch does. Built for the pilot who doesn't have 20 minutes to tab between apps.

**Dispatch** — Part 135 release decisions with the full compliance picture assembled automatically. Crew duty time, currency, aircraft airworthiness, FRAT — in one screen, not six. Every release is a timestamped, auditable record.

**MX Worker + Training Suite** — Maintenance logs and training records that follow the tail and the pilot, not the employer. AD compliance, component life, inspector sign-offs. Line checks, recurrents, proficiency checks — append-only, cryptographically anchored, producible in minutes during a certificate action.

**Studio Locker** — AI-generated GOM, SOP, and MEL drafts built from your actual configuration. Searchable regulatory library: CFRs, ADs, ACs, POHs — no more hunting through the FAA website at midnight before an audit.
