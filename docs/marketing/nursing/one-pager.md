# SOCIII for Nursing Schools

## Your Graduates Deserve Records That Travel With Them

A nursing degree proves you studied. A SOCIII competency record proves you can do the work. Every clinical sign-off, every skill attainment, every instructor endorsement — immutable, portable, yours for life. Not in a binder. Not in a shared inbox. In a personal Vault that goes with you after graduation.

This is what modern nursing education looks like.

Today, most programs track clinical competencies on paper forms that get scanned to PDFs and filed somewhere only the program administrator can find. When a graduate applies for her first ICU position, her transcript lists courses — it says nothing about whether she can place an IV or read a 12-lead ECG. ACEN and CCNE both require competency documentation. SOCIII makes meeting that standard the byproduct of doing the work, not a separate administrative burden.

---

### What SOCIII Does

- **Digital clinical sign-offs in 90 seconds.** Instructors complete and sign competency evaluations on any device. The signature is cryptographically anchored. The record cannot be altered.
- **ATI data flows in automatically.** No double-entry. Your ATI scores sync via LTI — SOCIII is the LTI Platform; ATI is the Tool. One source of truth.
- **Students keep their records for life.** Every competency attainment, clinical hour, and instructor sign-off lives in a personal Vault the student owns. It goes with them after graduation — to employers, licensing boards, graduate programs.
- **Audit-ready from day one.** Pull a complete, timestamped competency record for any student in seconds. No binders. No chasing down instructors for signatures from two semesters ago.
