## Slide 2: The Problem

**Your graduates cannot prove what they know how to do.**

- A nursing transcript lists courses. It does not list competencies.
- Clinical sign-offs live on paper forms, PDFs, and shared inboxes.
- When a student interviews for an ICU position, she has no verified record of clinical attainment — only her own word.
- When an accreditor asks for competency documentation, someone spends three weeks pulling binders.

## Slide 4: SOCIII Overview

**One record substrate. Three problems solved.**

SOCIII is a records infrastructure platform for nursing education. It is not an LMS replacement. It sits beneath your existing tools and makes their outputs permanent, portable, and verifiable.

**What it does:**
- Digitizes and cryptographically anchors clinical competency sign-offs
- Connects to ATI via LTI — no double-entry
- Gives every student a personal Vault they own for life
- Produces audit-ready documentation as a byproduct of normal workflow

**What it does not do:**
- Replace your LMS
- Change how instructors teach
- Require students to learn a new assessment system
