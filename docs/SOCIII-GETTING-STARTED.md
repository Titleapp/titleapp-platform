# SOCIII Getting Started Guide
## You're Five Minutes From Talking to Alex

Everything you need to start running your business through a conversation — no implementation project, no training week, no manual.

---

## What You're Looking At

SOCIII is an AI-powered operations platform. The short version: instead of logging into five different tools to run your business, you talk to Alex — and Alex works your tools for you.

There are two parts to the product:

- **Alex** — your Chief of Staff. You talk to Alex in plain English. Alex reads your context, answers questions, takes actions you approve, and routes work to the right Digital Worker.
- **Digital Workers** — specialized AI agents for specific jobs: title search, zoning analysis, student records, flight briefings, product compliance. Each one has a canvas (the visual panel on the right) that shows structured results, and a chat panel where Alex coordinates.

You don't need to learn the workers before you start. Alex will introduce them as they become relevant.

---

## Five Steps to Your First Conversation

**1. Sign in**
Go to sociii.ai and sign in with Google. No credit card required to start. No form to fill out about your business yet — Alex will ask you.

**2. Verify your identity (2 minutes)**
SOCIII holds sensitive business and personal records. Before you access the Vault or connect external accounts, you'll complete a one-time ID check via Stripe Identity — the same verification used by Airbnb, Lyft, and thousands of other platforms. Take a photo of your ID and a quick selfie. You won't need to do this again.

**3. Tell Alex about your business**
When you first open a workspace, Alex will ask a few questions: what kind of business you're running, your biggest operational headache right now, and whether there are accounts or tools you want to connect. Answer in plain English — you can skip any question and come back later.

**4. Connect your accounts (optional but powerful)**
SOCIII gets smarter when it can read your real data. You can connect:
- Google — Gmail, Calendar, Drive
- Microsoft — Outlook, OneDrive
- Property data — ATTOM (for real estate / title users)
- Accounting — connect your bank to seed your Accounting worker
- Aviation data — for flight operations users

Each account connects with a single OAuth click — SOCIII reads but never posts without your explicit approval, and you can disconnect at any time.

**5. Start talking to Alex**
Open the chat panel and say something like:
- "I'm a title agent handling three active orders right now. Can you help me track them?"
- "I run a small nursing program. Our biggest problem is tracking clinical hours for accreditation. What can you do?"
- "Pull up the zoning on 313 Mayfair Dr, Athens TX."

Alex will respond, ask any follow-up questions needed, and start working. When Alex takes an action on your behalf — sending an email, opening a title order, logging a record — you'll see an approval card before anything happens. You approve, Alex executes. Nothing happens without your confirmation.

## How Much Control Do You Want to Give Alex?

Alex starts conservative — it proposes every action and waits for your approval. As you get comfortable, you can expand its authorization for specific task types.

**Light — Propose and show me everything**
Alex drafts the email, shows it to you, and sends only when you tap Approve. Every action is visible before it happens.

**Middle — Handle the routine stuff, flag the exceptions**
"Alex, you can send routine follow-up emails to referral partners without asking me each time. Flag anything where the amount or counterparty is new." Alex handles the defined class of task autonomously and surfaces only the exceptions.

**Full — Just handle it**
"Alex, manage my title order pipeline. Open orders when a new file comes in, run the title search, and notify me when something needs a decision." Alex runs the workflow end-to-end, logs everything to the Vault, and brings you in only when a human decision is required.

Authorization is per-task-type, always logged, and revocable anytime.

## What You'll See in the Canvas

The panel on the right side of the screen is the canvas — a structured visual display of what your active Digital Worker knows. A Title Search worker shows chain-of-title, liens, and risk score. A Zoning worker shows the zoning verdict, permitted uses, and overlays. A Student Records worker shows enrollment, clinical hours, and NCLEX readiness. An HR worker shows your team roster, open roles, and upcoming reviews.

The canvas updates as Alex works. You don't need to refresh it or navigate anywhere — Alex tells you when something new is ready to look at.

On mobile: the chat works on any device. The canvas is optimized for desktop and tablet; on a phone it shifts to a card summary. For quick questions, checking status, or approving actions, your phone works fine.

## The More You Use It, the Smarter It Gets

SOCIII learns your context over time. After a few conversations, Alex knows your team structure, your active deals or cases, your communication style, and which workers you use most.

This is not general AI training — your data stays in your workspace. Alex is getting smarter about *your* business, not everyone else's.

## Build Your Own Worker

After you've used a few of SOCIII's built-in workers, you can create your own. Tell Alex: "I want to build a worker that tracks my referral partner relationships." Alex will walk you through it — what the worker should know, what it should do, what canvas it should show. No code required. You can keep it private, share it with your organization, or publish it to the SOCIII marketplace.

## Common First Questions

**Can Alex access my email without me knowing?**
No. Alex can read your email when you've connected Gmail or Outlook and asked Alex to check it. Alex will show you what it found and ask before taking any action — unless you've explicitly authorized it to handle a specific type of email autonomously.

**What happens to my data?**
Your workspace data — records, conversations, connected accounts — is stored in your tenant. It is not used to train AI models. It is not shared with other tenants.

**What if Alex gets something wrong?**
Tell it. Say "that's not right" and Alex will correct its understanding. Every factual statement Alex makes about your business data is grounded in records you've provided or approved — not invented.

**How do I get my team on it?**
From your workspace settings, invite team members by email. Each person signs in with their own Google account. You control their role and which workers they can access.

**Does it work on my phone?**
Yes. Open sociii.ai in any mobile browser and sign in. The chat is fully functional on mobile. For reviewing detailed reports or working through a deal, a desktop or tablet is the better experience.

**Need help?**
Tell Alex: "I'm not sure how to get started with the title workers." Alex knows the product and will walk you through it. Or email alex@sociii.ai.
