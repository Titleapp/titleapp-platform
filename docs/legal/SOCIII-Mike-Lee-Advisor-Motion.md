# SOCIII Inc.
# Written Consent of Members / Manager Approval
# Addition of Mike Lee to Advisory Board

**Date:** July 2026
**Entity:** SOCIII Inc.
**Action:** Appointment of Mike Lee as Advisor; grant of advisory equity interest

---

## Recitals

The undersigned, being the Manager / sole founding member of SOCIII Inc. (the "Company"), hereby approves the following actions by written consent in lieu of a formal meeting, effective as of the date last signed below.

---

## Resolutions

**RESOLVED**, that Mike Lee ("Advisor") is hereby appointed to the Advisory Board of SOCIII Inc., effective upon execution of the Advisor Agreement and Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement described below.

**RESOLVED**, that the Company is authorized to enter into an Advisor Agreement with Mike Lee, pursuant to which Advisor will provide business development, customer introductions, and strategic guidance to the Company.

**RESOLVED**, that Sean Combs, as founding member, shall transfer from his personal equity holdings a 2% membership interest (or equivalent equity unit) in SOCIII Inc. to Mike Lee as advisory compensation, subject to the vesting schedule set forth in the Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement. This transfer is sourced entirely from Sean Combs's personal holdings and does not dilute the Company's equity pool.

**RESOLVED**, that the officers and managers of the Company are authorized to execute, deliver, and perform all documents and instruments necessary or appropriate to implement the foregoing resolutions, including the Advisor Agreement, the Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement, and any required cap table amendments.

---

## Basis for Appointment

Mike Lee has introduced SOCIII Inc. to Attorneys Title Company of Henderson County (Athens, TX) — specifically to Troy Garris (Garris Horn LLP) and Chad Hardgrave (Benchmark Mortgage, NMLS #2143) — a prospective customer for the SOCIII Title Production Suite. Mike's relationships in the Texas title and commercial real estate markets represent material business development value to the Company.

---

## Terms of Advisory Equity

- **Equity amount:** 2% of the Company, transferred from Sean Combs's personal holdings
- **Vesting:** Standard 2-year monthly vesting with 6-month cliff (subject to RSPA terms)
- **Grant date:** Date of signing of the RSPA by both parties
- **83(b) election:** Advisor is responsible for timely filing of 83(b) election with the IRS within 30 days of grant date if applicable. Company will provide standard notice.
- **Source:** Sean Combs's personal equity — Company capitalization structure unchanged.

---

## Documents to be Prepared and Executed

1. **Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement (RSPA)** — based on standard SOCIII advisor template; grant date = date of Advisor's signature
2. **Advisor Agreement** — specifying advisory role, meeting cadence, and equity as sole compensation
3. **Cap table amendment** — to be filed with the Company's records and legal counsel
