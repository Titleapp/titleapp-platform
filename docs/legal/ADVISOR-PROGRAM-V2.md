# SOCIII Advisor Program v2 – Working Advisors

**Effective:** July 2026
**Entity:** SOCIII Inc.

---

## Philosophy

SOCIII advisors are not honorary board members or passive names on a website. Advisors are expected to be active participants in the growth of the company — using the platform, providing market intelligence, opening strategic relationships, and serving as reference customers.

**AI does the work. Humans build trust.**

AI performs the operational work. Advisors help build the relationships, credibility, and market access that AI cannot.

---

## Advisor Responsibilities

### 1. Be a Customer First

Every advisor is expected to onboard using the standard SOCIII customer experience, including account creation, subscription, and payment.

SOCIII will reimburse approved subscription fees during the advisory relationship, but advisors should experience the platform exactly as a paying customer.

This ensures advisors can provide meaningful feedback on:

- Onboarding
- Billing
- User experience
- Product quality
- Customer journey

If an advisor cannot comfortably recommend SOCIII after using it themselves, they should not be recommending it to others.

### 2. Strategic Relationships

Advisors are expected to make reasonable efforts to introduce SOCIII to customers, partners, investors, regulators, and industry leaders where appropriate.

The value of an advisor is measured by the quality of relationships opened — not the number of meetings attended.

### 3. Product Feedback

Advisors should periodically provide executive-level feedback on:

- Market needs
- Competitive landscape
- Industry trends
- Product direction

### 4. Reference Customer

Whenever practical, advisors should become active users of the platform within their own business and be willing to serve as reference customers or case studies.

---

## Equity

Advisor equity is earned by creating strategic enterprise value.

While every advisor is expected to contribute through product usage, introductions, and feedback, **additional equity grants should be tied to measurable outcomes** such as:

- Strategic partnerships
- Enterprise customer acquisition
- Platform integrations
- Other milestones that materially increase the value of SOCIII

Base advisory equity reflects the commitment to serve in the role. Milestone-based grants recognize exceptional contribution beyond baseline expectations.

---

## Advisor Success Plan

Each advisor will have an individual Success Plan based on their network and expertise. Success Plans are documented at the time of appointment and reviewed periodically.

### Example: Mike Lee

**Domain:** Commercial finance, capital markets, Texas title and CRE markets

**Responsibilities:**
- Commercial finance and capital markets introductions
- Enterprise customer introductions (title companies, mortgage firms, CRE operators)
- Strategic banking relationships
- Active SOCIII customer (Title Production Suite)

### Example: Frontier AI Advisor

**Domain:** AI ecosystem (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Databricks)

**Responsibilities:**
- Strategic AI ecosystem relationships
- Executive introductions at frontier AI labs and enterprise AI buyers
- Product feedback from the perspective of AI infrastructure and deployment
- Active SOCIII customer

---

## Core Principle

SOCIII is building an AI-native company.

AI should perform the operational work.

Humans should focus on trust, relationships, partnerships, and strategy.

Every advisor should strengthen the network effect of the platform by being both a customer and an advocate.

---

## Program Administration

- Advisor agreements are executed via the standard RSPA + Advisor Agreement template
- Subscription reimbursements are processed monthly upon receipt of invoice or platform usage confirmation
- Success Plans are reviewed annually or upon material change in the advisor's role
- Equity vesting and 83(b) obligations are governed by the RSPA terms specific to each advisor
