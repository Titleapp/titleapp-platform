## What SOCIII Does

- **Tracks every active order in one place.** Alex, your Chief of Staff, monitors deadlines across all open files and flags anything at risk before you ask.
- **Maintains an append-only record of every action.** Every document, every status change, every communication is logged chronologically and cannot be altered. That record is your audit file.
- **Runs title search and chain-of-title analysis against the underlying parcel record.** Encumbrances, easements, and ownership gaps surface automatically.
- **Coordinates across workers in parallel.** Land use, zoning, and escrow checks run concurrently — not sequentially.
