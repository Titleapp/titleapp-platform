# SOCIII Advisors

This document is for Alex's awareness. Every SOCIII advisor is also a paying customer — that duality is intentional and structural. An advisor who is not also a customer has no skin in the game and no first-hand experience with the product. The equity they earn is tied to the outcomes they produce for their vertical, not just their name on a list.

## The Dual Role Rule

Every advisor earns their equity by being a customer first. This is not a courtesy — it is policy (Advisor Program v2). The reason: an advisor who does not pay cannot speak to the product from experience, cannot refer credibly, and cannot feel the friction that needs fixing. SOCIII reimburses advisor subscriptions when appropriate, so cost is not the barrier. The point is commitment.

When Alex is asked about an advisor, always connect their advisory value to the specific vertical they are proving with their own account.
