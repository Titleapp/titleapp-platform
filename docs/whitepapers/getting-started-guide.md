# Getting Started with SOCIII
## You're five minutes from talking to Alex.

---

### What you're looking at

SOCIII is an AI-powered operations platform. The short version: instead of logging into five different tools to run your business, you talk to Alex — and Alex works your tools for you.

There are two parts to the product:

1. **Alex** — your AI operations coordinator. You talk to Alex in plain English. Alex reads your context, answers questions, takes actions you approve, and routes work to the right Digital Worker.

2. **Digital Workers** — specialized AI agents for specific jobs: title search, zoning analysis, student records, flight briefings, product compliance. Each one has a canvas (the visual panel on the right) that shows structured results, and a chat panel where Alex coordinates.

You don't need to learn the workers before you start. Alex will introduce them as they become relevant.

---

### Step 1 — Sign in

Go to **sociii.ai** and sign in with Google.

That's it. No credit card required to start. No form to fill out about your business yet — Alex will ask you.

---

### Step 2 — Verify your identity (takes 2 minutes)

SOCIII holds sensitive business and personal records. Before you access the Vault or connect external accounts, you'll complete a one-time ID check via Stripe Identity — the same verification used by Airbnb, Lyft, and thousands of other platforms.

Take a photo of your ID and a quick selfie. Done. You won't need to do this again.

---

### Step 3 — Tell Alex about your business

When you first open a workspace, Alex will ask you a few questions:

- What kind of business are you running?
- What's your biggest operational headache right now?
- Are there accounts or tools you want to connect?

Answer in plain English. Alex uses your answers to set up your workspace and suggest the right Digital Workers to start with.

You can skip any question and come back later. Your answers are not locked in.

---

### Step 4 — Connect your accounts (optional but powerful)

SOCIII gets smarter when it can read your real data. You can connect:

- **Google** — Gmail, Calendar, Drive
- **Microsoft** — Outlook, OneDrive
- **Property data** — ATTOM (for real estate / title users)
- **Accounting** — connect your bank to seed your Accounting worker
- **Aviation data** — for flight operations users

You connect each account with a single OAuth click — SOCIII reads but never posts without your explicit approval. You can disconnect any account at any time.

If you'd rather start without connecting anything, that works too. Alex will work with whatever you share directly in the chat.

---

### Step 5 — Start talking to Alex

Open the chat panel and say something like:

> "I'm a title agent in Henderson County TX. I have three active orders right now. Can you help me track them?"

Or:

> "I run a small nursing program. Our biggest problem is tracking clinical hours for accreditation. What can you do?"

Or:

> "Pull up the zoning on 313 Mayfair Dr, Athens TX."

Alex will respond, ask any follow-up questions needed, and start working. When Alex takes an action on your behalf — sending an email, opening a title order, logging a record — you'll see an approval card before anything happens. You approve, Alex executes. Nothing happens without your confirmation.

---

### How much control do you want to give Alex?

Alex starts conservative — it proposes every action and waits for your approval. As you get comfortable, you can expand its authorization for specific task types. Think of it as a dial from light to full:

**Light — propose and show me everything:**
Alex drafts the email, shows it to you, and sends only when you tap Approve. Alex runs the title search, shows you the results, and logs them only when you confirm. Every action is visible before it happens.

**Middle — handle the routine stuff, flag the exceptions:**
> "Alex, you can send routine follow-up emails to referral partners without asking me each time. Flag anything where the amount or counterparty is new."

Alex handles the defined class of task autonomously and surfaces only the exceptions that need your judgment.

**Full — just handle it:**
> "Alex, manage my title order pipeline. Open orders when a new file comes in, run the title search, and notify me when something needs a decision."

Alex runs the workflow end-to-end, logs everything to the Vault, and brings you in only when a human decision is required. You review a summary, not a queue of approval cards.

Authorization is per-task-type, always logged, and revocable anytime. You set the level that fits how you work — and you can change it at any time by telling Alex.

### What you'll see in the canvas

The panel on the right side of the screen is the canvas — a structured visual display of what your active Digital Worker knows. Each worker has tabs:

- A **Title Search worker** shows chain-of-title, liens, and risk score.
- A **Zoning worker** shows the zoning verdict, permitted uses, and overlays.
- A **Student Records worker** shows enrollment, clinical hours, and NCLEX readiness.
- An **HR worker** shows your team roster, open roles, and upcoming reviews.

The canvas updates as Alex works. You don't need to refresh it or navigate anywhere — Alex tells you when something new is ready to look at.

**On mobile:** The chat works on any device — open your browser, sign in, and start talking. The canvas (the structured visual panel) is optimized for desktop and tablet. On a phone, it shifts to a card summary below your conversation so the key results are still visible. If you're doing detailed review work — reading a title report, reviewing a pro forma — a larger screen is worth it. For quick questions, checking status, or approving actions, your phone is fine.

### The more you use it, the smarter it gets

SOCIII learns your context over time. After a few conversations, Alex knows:

- Your team structure (who handles what)
- Your active deals, orders, or cases
- Your communication style (brief vs. detailed)
- Which workers you use most

This is not general AI training — your data stays in your workspace. Alex is getting smarter about *your* business, not everyone else's.

### If you want to go deeper: build your own worker

After you've used a few of SOCIII's built-in workers, you can create your own. Tell Alex:

> "I want to build a worker that tracks my referral partner relationships."

Alex will walk you through it: what the worker should know, what it should do, what canvas it should show. No code required. The worker you build lives in your workspace and is available to your whole team.

You can keep it private, share it with your organization, or publish it to the SOCIII marketplace for other operators to use.

### Common first questions

**"Can Alex access my email without me knowing?"**
No. Alex can read your email when you've connected Gmail or Outlook and asked Alex to check it. Alex will show you what it found and ask before taking any action — unless you've explicitly authorized it to handle a specific type of email autonomously.

**"What happens to my data?"**
Your workspace data — records, conversations, connected accounts — is stored in your tenant. It is not used to train AI models. It is not shared with other tenants.

**"What if Alex gets something wrong?"**
Tell it. Say "that's not right" or "the address is different" and Alex will correct its understanding. Every factual statement Alex makes about your business data is grounded in records you've provided or approved — not invented.

**"How do I get my team on it?"**
From your workspace settings, invite team members by email. Each person signs in with their own Google account. You control their role (admin vs. member) and which workers they can access.

**"Does it work on my phone?"**
Yes. Open sociii.ai in any mobile browser and sign in. The chat is fully functional on mobile. The structured canvas panels are better on a larger screen — for reviewing detailed reports or working through a deal, a desktop or tablet is the better experience. The quick stuff — checking status, approving an action, asking Alex a question — works great on a phone.
